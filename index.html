01 / NEXT UP

Gymbell quietly studies your training patterns — frequency, fatigue, time since last hit — and surfaces tomorrow's session before you ask. No spreadsheets, no scheduling.

02 / IN THE ZONE

Stepper controls for reps and weight, progressive overload hints when the bar's ready to move, and an auto rest timer that knows when you're back. Eyes on the lift, not the phone.

  • Real-time hints — push +2.5 kg, hold reps

03 / BODY BALANCE

Progress faster.

A 30-day muscle-group readout reveals what you've been skipping — every back day you skipped, every neglected hamstring. Even pull, even push, even legs.

  • Volume per group, rolling 30 days

  • Filter by lift family or movement plane

§ 08 Questions & Contact

Anything else
we should answer?

What happens after the 1-month trial?
Nothing surprising. Your trial simply ends and you choose Monthly ($2.99) or Lifetime ($39.99) from inside the app. No auto-charge surprises — the App Store handles billing transparently.
Which AI providers does Gymbell support?
You bring your own API key — currently Apple Intelligence, Claude (Anthropic), OpenAI, and Google Gemini, plus any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Your key is stored locally in iOS Keychain and used only to call the provider directly.
Can I import data from another app?
Yes — Strong CSV imports are supported out of the box. Drop the file from Files or share it from Strong; Gymbell maps exercises automatically.
Do I need the Apple Watch app to use Gymbell?
No. Gymbell works fully on iPhone alone. The Watch app is a free companion that mirrors the active session for hands-off logging.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled through Apple — open the App Store, tap your profile → Purchase History, then Report a Problem. We're happy to vouch for any genuine issue; just email us first and we'll help.
